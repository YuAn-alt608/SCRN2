[MUST BE REPORTED HONESTLY] directional conflict
  Every MR estimate (Wald ratio) is positive:
    SCRN2 expression up -> TSH up (+0.027) / hypothyroidism up (+0.070) / Hashimoto up (+0.176)
    i.e. high SCRN2 expression = higher disease risk, low expression = protective

  CTD direction: EDC exposure -> SCRN2 expression DOWN

  Combining the two: EDC lowers SCRN2 -> hypothyroidism risk FALLS
  This contradicts the public-health expectation that EDCs harm the thyroid.

  Three honest readings (to be discussed as they stand, not forced into a closed loop):
   (1) most CTD records are high-dose, in vitro or cross-species acute stress responses,
       and do not represent the net effect of chronic low-dose exposure in humans;
   (2) the thyroid toxicity of EDCs may not be mediated THROUGH SCRN2 -
       SCRN2 is an EDC-responsive gene, but its response and its pathogenicity point in
       decoupled directions;
   (3) thyroid feedback makes it hard to extrapolate a one-directional expression change
       linearly to disease.

  -> Conclusion: the "EDC-responsive gene" identity of SCRN2 stands (the CTD evidence is
      substantial), but its causal role in mediating EDC thyroid toxicity is NOT
      supported by the present evidence. In the manuscript it is downgraded to a
      candidate mechanistic lead rather than a demonstrated causal chain.
